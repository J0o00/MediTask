🧪 MediTask dataset recorder

Records labelled, automatically segmented repetitions for training the temporal model (the "MediTask custom" source). Video never leaves this device; only skeleton coordinates are exported, to a file you download.

Research use only. Obtain institutional approval and informed consent before recording participants. Use anonymous subject IDs (e.g. S07) - never names. Record healthy volunteers first; have a physiotherapist confirm each label. Do not ask people with injuries to perform deliberately incorrect movements.
