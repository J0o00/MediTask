Records labelled, automatically segmented repetitions for training the temporal model. Video never leaves this device; only skeleton coordinates are exported, to a file you download.

Research use only. Obtain institutional approval and informed consent before recording. Use anonymous subject IDs such as S07, never names. Record healthy volunteers first, have a physiotherapist confirm each label, and do not ask people with injuries to perform deliberately incorrect movements.
