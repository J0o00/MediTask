    Before you start

    • MediTask uses your camera to track body movement. Video is processed on this device only and is never uploaded or stored.
    • Only movement measurements (joint angles, repetitions, quality scores) and the pain/difficulty you report are saved and shared with your linked physiotherapist.
    • MediTask supports your physiotherapist. It does not diagnose conditions or change your treatment.
    • Stop immediately if an exercise causes pain, dizziness or breathlessness, and contact your physiotherapist. In an emergency call 112.
