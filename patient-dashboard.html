    Before your first session

    MediTask uses your camera to track body movement. Video is processed on this device only and is never uploaded or stored.

    Only movement measurements (joint angles, repetitions, quality scores) and the pain and difficulty you report are saved and shared with your physiotherapist.

    MediTask supports your physiotherapist. It does not diagnose conditions or change your treatment.

    Stop immediately if an exercise causes pain, dizziness or breathlessness. In an emergency call 112.
