MediTask

Home rehabilitation, supervised by your physiotherapist.

Exercise in front of your camera and get guidance on every repetition. Your physiotherapist sees how you are progressing between visits.

  • Video stays on your device. Only movement measurements are shared.
  • Repetitions, range of motion and form checked in real time.
  • Prescriptions, progress and messages in one place for patient and clinician.

MediTask supports clinical care. It does not diagnose or replace your physiotherapist.
